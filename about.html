About MSSM

A Legacy of Education.

Established in 2001 under the aegis of the Indian Institute of Engineering Technology Society, MSSM is affiliated to the University of Madras and approved by AICTE.

Our Story

From legacy to management education.

1947 — Legacy begins

The Meenakshi Sundararajan Group of Institutions carries a legacy of educational service dating back to 1947, founded on the vision of Late Shri. K.R. Sundararajan.

2001 — MSSM established

MSSM was established under the aegis of IIET, with affiliation to the University of Madras and approval from AICTE.

Today — The MSSM experience

Management education + industry exposure + innovation + student development.
