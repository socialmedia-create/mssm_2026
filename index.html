360° LEARN FRAMEWORK

One MBA. A 360° Learning Journey.

At MSSM, learning doesn't stop when the class ends. Our 360° LEARN Framework connects concepts, collaboration, application, reflection and professional exposure across the student's journey.

L

LEARN

Build the thinking. Concepts are explored through flipped classrooms, cases, discussion, AI-enabled learning and continuous assessment.

From: “I know the concept.”
To: “I understand why it matters.”
E

ENGAGE

Learn with others. Students debate, collaborate, solve problems, present, compete and create—because management is never a solo profession.

From: Individual learning
To: Collaborative intelligence.
A

APPLY

Put knowledge to work. Live projects, field assignments, industry interactions, boot camps, visits and classroom simulations turn management concepts into decisions.

From: “I learned it.”
To: “I can use it.”
R

REFLECT

Learn from what you do. Students receive feedback, evaluate decisions, identify gaps and improve.

From: Experience
To: Learning from experience.
N

NETWORK

Build relationships that matter. Corporate interactions, conclaves, alumni engagement, clubs, competitions and institutional connections place students inside a larger professional ecosystem.

From: A classroom network
To: A professional network.
THE PEDAGOGY

How do we make learning happen? EPIC.

Our pedagogy is not a collection of activities. It is a system.

E

Experiential Learning & Immersive Feedback

Students learn by doing—and improve through feedback.

P

Pedagogy & Continuous Learning

Every classroom moves beyond one-way teaching through flipped learning, cases, discussion, AI-enabled learning and active participation.

I

Institutional Framework & Corporate Engagement

Academic learning connects with MSSM's Centres, corporate partners and industry ecosystem.

C

Campus Life & Networking System

Clubs, competitions, student-led initiatives, events and peer interactions turn campus into another learning environment.

CONCEPT→EXPERIENCE→FEEDBACK→APPLICATION→REFLECTION→PROFESSIONAL READINESS

360° LEARN is the journey. EPIC is the way we deliver it.
