Student Support & Grievance Redressal

Every concern deserves to be heard.

MSSM is committed to maintaining a respectful, transparent and supportive academic environment where students can raise concerns and seek appropriate guidance.

Student Support

Support when you need it.

Clear institutional pathways help students seek guidance, communicate concerns and access the appropriate support mechanism.

01

Academic Support

Guidance relating to academic concerns, learning and student progress.

02

Counselling & Mentoring

Support for students navigating academic, personal and professional development.

03

Grievance Redressal

A structured mechanism for receiving, reviewing and addressing student grievances fairly and promptly.

Grievance Redressal

A clear route for student concerns.

The grievance mechanism can cover concerns relating to academic processes, administration, campus facilities, student support and other matters requiring institutional attention.

Academic

Learning, assessment or academic-process concerns.

Administration

Student services and administrative processes.

Campus

Facilities, safety or student-support matters.

Other Concerns

Any issue that needs an appropriate institutional channel.

Need assistance?

Start with the right institutional contact.

For official grievance committee details, forms, contact information and the current procedure, refer to the latest institutional notice or contact the MSSM administration.
